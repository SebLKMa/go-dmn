# Sample Contract Clause: Volume Discount

## Section X. Pricing and Volume Discounts.  
(a) Base Pricing. The Buyer agrees to purchase Goods at the initial base wholesale price of $50.00 per unit, as set forth in the Product Catalog.  

(b) Discount Tiers. Discount percentages are determined by the cumulative volume of units purchased by the Buyer within a single twelve (12) month contract cycle. Tiers will be applied as follows:  
- Silver Tier: Cumulative purchases of 500 to 1,499 units qualify for a 5% discount on all subsequent orders within that contract cycle.  
- Gold Tier: Cumulative purchases of 1,500 to 4,999 units qualify for a 10% discount on all subsequent orders within that contract cycle.  
- Platinum Tier: Cumulative purchases of 5,000 or more units qualify for a 15% discount on all subsequent orders within that contract cycle.  

(c) Verification & Adjustments. Pricing tier qualifications are reviewed at the end of each fiscal quarter. If the Buyer moves into a higher tier, the updated discount applies to all orders placed after the date of verification. Discounts are non-retroactive for units already invoiced and paid.
